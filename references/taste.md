# Taste log (what the owner accepted / rejected)

Accepted
- Technical Minimal theme on the TV launcher: Doto clock and wordmark, mono index labels, hue
  swatches, hairlines, dot grid, side panels with kinetic rows.
- Coloured glow on the focused card (LeanBox glow) — but subtle (ambient only, ~16dp) and in
  the colour of the ICON, not of the banner.
- Line-style system icons with the same meaning as the originals.
- Weather + date top right, bigger (48dp) status buttons.
- Real app banners and icons on cards (recognisability first).
- Icon pack "faithful": original icons identical, only squircle + hairline + LED; app name
  written by the launcher under the icon.

Rejected
- App icons redrawn as single dot-matrix letters/symbols ("non si capisce che app sono").
- Rendering real logos as low-resolution LED dot panels ("si vedono male").
- Too strong glow; glow colour taken from the banner instead of the icon.
- Anything that requires long-press or Home on a TV remote.

Process
- Show a preview (screenshot / contact sheet) before rolling out a visual change.
- Don't send keys to a device the owner is using; check the foreground app first.
